Common questions

Clear answers for common enquiries. Project-specific requirements are confirmed during quote review.

What systems do you provide?

We provide fire detection and protection, CCTV, electrical, plumbing and LPG systems, including design, supply, installation, testing, commissioning and maintenance as required by the project scope.

Do you handle maintenance after installation?

Yes. Our Service & Maintenance Department supports ongoing maintenance and review. The exact maintenance scope depends on the installed system and site requirements.

Can you share project references or approvals?

Project references, photographs, certifications and authority approvals can be shared when confirmed and authorized for publication. We do not publish unverified claims.

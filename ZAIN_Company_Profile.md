## COMPANY OVERVIEW

Established in **2021**, **ZAIN TECHNICAL & INTEGRATED SERVICES LLC** is an Engineering, Contracting and Service Provider Company with a wide choice for Fire Detection and Fire Protection Systems. We offer a wide range of services from design, supply, installation, maintenance, testing and commissioning of fire detection and fire protection systems. These services are well supported by our several departments including the Service and Maintenance Department and Engineering.

Our company, ZAIN TECHNICAL & INTEGRATED SERVICES LLC, follows strict globally accepted standards in providing Fire Safety Solutions for developers, retailers and businesses throughout Oman. The services & solutions we provide are individually tailored to our client's needs & take account of their objectives for statutory compliance, duty of care and protection of their business. Regular inspection & review of all Fire Safety Systems and Equipment is of paramount importance. Our ability to back it up with comprehensive training, efficient maintenance & thorough system know-how has greatly reduced the risks among our various clients.

**Plumbing Maintenance Checklist:**
- **Faucets & Shower Heads** – Check interior & exterior faucets for leaks. Clean aerators. Replace washers if necessary.
- **Water Heater** – Check area around water heater for leaks. If you have hard water, drain 1-2 gallons of water.
- **Kitchen & Bathroom Cabinets** – Check under and around for leaks.
- **Drains** – Clean with baking soda. Pour water down unused drains.
- **Pipes** – Inspect visible pipes for leaks.
- **Toilets** – Check for stability and leaks.
